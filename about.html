Our Journey

How We Started a Fresh Movement

FreshMarket began with a simple observation: supermarket produce took days to reach shelves, losing nutrients and flavor along the way, while local organic farmers struggled to find direct markets.

We bridged this gap by creating a direct farm-to-home logistics pipeline. Today, we deliver to over 12,000 households daily while supporting 50+ regional organic farm families.

2018 — The Beginning
Started with 3 Partner Farms in Ooty
Delivered 50 boxes a week to local neighbors using a single pickup van.
2021 — Cold-Chain Expansion
100% Refrigerated Transport
Invested in temperature-controlled fleet, extending delivery coverage across 80 pincodes.
2024 — Zero-Pesticide Promise
Full Organic Certification
Partnered with certified organic verification agencies. 100% pesticide-free audit guarantee.
2026 — 12,000+ Families
Leading Local Produce Brand
Serving 12,000+ daily customers with 500+ fresh SKUs directly from 50+ partner farms.
What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Purity First

We never compromise on organic integrity. Zero synthetic pesticides, chemical ripening, or wax coatings on any produce we deliver.

Fair Farmer Prices

Farmers receive 70% of retail price — far above standard market rates. When you shop with us, you directly sustain local agricultural communities.

Zero Plastic Waste

100% plastic-free packaging. All deliveries use reusable jute bags and compostable paper wraps to protect our planet.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if a product is damaged or not fresh? +
We offer a 100% no-questions-asked freshness guarantee. Send us a photo via WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery and we will either issue an immediate replacement or refund your account.
How does the weekly subscription work? +
You choose your box size (Solo, Family, or Feast) and select your preferred delivery day. Your card will be charged weekly, and you can pause, skip a week, or cancel anytime through your dashboard with zero penalty.
What is the cutoff time for same-day delivery? +
Order before 10 AM for evening delivery (5 PM – 9 PM). Orders placed after 10 AM will be delivered the following morning (6 AM – 10 AM). Express 2-hour delivery is available until 7 PM daily.
Are all your products 100% certified organic? +
Yes! All produce comes from our network of 50+ certified organic farms. Every batch undergoes weekly lab testing for zero pesticide residues. Lab reports are available upon request.
Do you offer wholesale pricing for restaurants? +
Yes, we serve over 150 restaurants, cafes, and catering companies. Wholesale accounts receive up to 40% off retail pricing, 6 AM early morning kitchen delivery, and 15/30 day credit terms. Submit a quote request on our Shop page.

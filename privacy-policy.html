1. Privacy Summary & Promise

Our Core Privacy Promise: FreshMarket will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers. We only collect the bare minimum data required to deliver your fresh produce orders reliably.

This Privacy Policy explains how FreshMarket ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you visit our website, mobile site, or use our delivery services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being associated with you ("Personal Data"):

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Details: Name, delivery address, pincode, email address, phone number.
  • Order Details: Produce selected, delivery slot preferences, order history, bulk quote requests.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay, Stripe). We do not store raw card numbers.
  • Communications: Customer support messages, feedback, ratings, and subscription preferences.

B. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, time spent.
  • Location Data: Approximate delivery zone location based on IP or pincode input.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data solely for legitimate business purposes:

  • To process, pack, and deliver your daily fresh produce orders.
  • To send order confirmation, live delivery tracking SMS/WhatsApp updates.
  • To manage weekly subscription preferences and billing.
  • To respond to customer service enquiries and process freshness returns.
  • To send optional weekly newsletters (only with your explicit opt-in).
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent transactions.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share data strictly with trusted service providers necessary to operate our business:

  • Delivery Partners: Our internal driver team and certified local couriers receive your name, address, and phone number for delivery fulfillment only.
  • Payment Processors: Encrypted transaction data sent securely to PCI-DSS certified gateways.
  • IT Infrastructure: Cloud hosting (AWS, Cloudflare) compliant with ISO 27001 standards.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use essential functional cookies to remember your theme preference (Dark/Light mode), layout direction (LTR/RTL), and cart items. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) and applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or update your personal information anytime.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and order history.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails using the "Unsubscribe" link in any email.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

If you have any questions, privacy concerns, or data deletion requests, contact our Data Protection Officer:
